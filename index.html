(03) Learning

Agentic AI roadmap

The 16-module path I'm following, from neural network basics to deploying LLM applications. Tap a module to see its topics.

  1. 1Feedforward Neural Networks

    Perceptrons and activations, layers and MLPs, the training loop, backpropagation by hand, and an MNIST digit classifier.

  2. 2Transformers: concepts

    Seq2Seq with RNNs, attention, self-attention, multi-head attention, positional encoding, residuals, encoders and decoders.

  3. 3Hugging Face and transfer learning

    Model Hub, pipelines, tokenizers, AutoModel, datasets, Trainer API, LoRA, quantization, QLoRA, and supervised fine-tuning.

  4. 4Introduction to Gen AI

    Foundation models, how an LLM interaction works, and LangChain: runnables, prompt templates, structured output, LCEL, and tool use.

  5. 5Prompt engineering

    Prompt components, data analysis with prompts, natural-language querying with Text2SQL, and intelligent document processing.

  6. 6Embeddings and vector databases

    How embeddings evolved, similarity metrics, types of search, and popular vector databases.

  7. 7Retrieval Augmented Generation

    When and why to use RAG, its challenges, chunking, and LangChain loaders, splitters, vector stores, and retrievers.

  8. 8RAG projects

    Image search with RAG and a review chatbot built with Gradio.

  9. 9RAG variations

    Re-ranking, query decomposition, HyDE, agentic RAG, hybrid search, metadata filtering, and context compression.

  10. 10Local large language models

    Ollama CLI, API, SDK, and Modelfile, plus prompt engineering and RAG projects with local small language models.

  11. 11Agentic AI

    CrewAI agents, tasks, crews, and processes, human-in-the-loop, tools, memory, knowledge bases, and a background verification project.

  12. 12Agentic patterns

    Reflection, ReAct, plan and execute, orchestrator and specialist, evaluator-optimizer, adversarial debate, and a fact-checking system.

  13. 13Low-code agentic AI

    MCP servers, agent skills, and LangGraph: nodes, edges, state, conditional routing, checkpoints, and persistence.

  14. 14Evaluation and observability

    DeepEval metrics, guardrails, LLM as a judge, LangSmith, and AgentOps.

  15. 15Containerize your LLM solution

    Docker images, containers, networks, and volumes, plus semantic response caching and router architecture to cut costs.

  16. 16Deploy your LLM solution

    Reviewer agents as guardrails, prompt versioning, and deployment on Vercel, Cloud Run, and Hugging Face Spaces.
